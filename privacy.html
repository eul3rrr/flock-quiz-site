Flock Quiz privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Flock Quiz is a bird identification quiz for Android. This page explains what the app does with data, which is very little.

What the app stores

Your quiz rating, streaks, favourite birds and the birds you have been shown are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account, sign-in or leaderboard.

What the app downloads

Every bird's photo ships inside the app, so it works offline. When your device is online, the app downloads a larger copy of the same photo from Wikimedia Commons (the Wikimedia Foundation's media library). Like any download, that request shows Wikimedia your IP address and the file requested; it is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy. Tapping a bird's name or the photo credit opens Wikipedia or Wikimedia Commons in your browser.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • No personal information is collected, stored or shared.
  • No permissions beyond internet access.

Children

The app is a general-audience quiz and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data (for example optional ads or in-app tips), this policy and the store listing will be updated first.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact on the Google Play listing.
