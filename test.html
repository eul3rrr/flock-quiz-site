Become a Flock Quiz tester

Flock Quiz is a bird identification quiz for Android. Before it can go on Google Play it needs testers for two weeks. Two taps on your Android phone and you're in.

Use the same Google account for both steps: the one your phone's Play Store is signed into.

1. Join the tester group

This puts your Google account on the tester list. Do it now, even if you only have a minute.

Join the group

Tap "Join group" on the page that opens. The group sends no emails.

2. Get the app

Google takes up to an hour after you join the group to unlock this, so come back later if it says the app is unavailable. Then tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play" and install.

Open the test page

Then keep the app installed for at least 14 days and play whenever you like. Spotted something broken or confusing? Send a screenshot to the person who invited you. Thank you!
